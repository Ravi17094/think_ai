# Instructor Onboarding Video Script

## Recording goal

Create a three-minute 1080p instructor onboarding video with captions and chapter markers.

| Time | Chapter | Presenter action | Narration cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 - 0:20 | Login | Sign in with the Instructor account. | "Welcome to Thinkz AI. This short guide shows how to prepare and run a live class." |
| 0:20 - 0:55 | Dashboard | Show dashboard totals and the upcoming session. | "The dashboard gives instructors a quick view of assigned work and upcoming teaching activity." |
| 0:55 - 1:30 | Schedule | Open the prepared session and show schedule details. | "Open a scheduled session to review class details before learners join." |
| 1:30 - 2:10 | Preparation | Open the checklist and complete one item. | "Use the preparation checklist to confirm audio, materials, breakout rooms, polls, and recording settings." |
| 2:10 - 2:45 | Go live | Open Live Studio and show chat, attendance, and controls. | "When preparation is complete, start Live Studio to manage participation and communicate with learners." |
| 2:45 - 3:00 | Close | Return to dashboard/report view. | "After the session, review attendance and progress reports from the instructor workspace." |

## Export checklist

- Record at 1080p with clear narration.
- Add captions and the chapter markers above.
- Remove personal information, passwords, API keys, and real learner data.
- Export as `instructor-onboarding-video.mp4`.
- Upload only to the client-approved hosting platform.
- Add the approved hosted URL to the instructor onboarding checklist.

## Status

Script complete. Recording, editing, upload, and final link require a presenter and client-approved hosting access.
