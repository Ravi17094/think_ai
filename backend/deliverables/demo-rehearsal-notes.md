# Thinkz AI Day 7 Demo Rehearsal Notes

## Goal

Complete the combined demo in 12 minutes or less. Use approved test accounts and a staging environment only.

## Instructor flow - target 5 minutes

1. Sign in as an Instructor and open the dashboard.
2. Schedule or open the prepared demo session.
3. Open the session preparation checklist and mark a prepared item.
4. Start Live Studio and confirm the session status.
5. Mark learner attendance in the attendance register and save.
6. Create a breakout room, assign a learner, then end the breakout.
7. End the session and open the attendance/progress report.

## RBAC flow - target 3 minutes

1. Sign in as Admin and open the RBAC matrix.
2. Change one non-critical demo permission and show the confirmation dialog.
3. Save the change and show the audit-log entry.
4. Sign in as the affected role and show the guard allows or denies the relevant page.
5. Restore the original permission after the demonstration.

## TA grading flow - target 3 minutes

1. Sign in as a TA and open the grading queue.
2. Open one submission, enter a score and feedback, then save.
3. Select two prepared submissions for bulk grading.
4. Apply the feedback template and confirm the bulk action.
5. Refresh the analytics/report view to confirm the updated outcome.
